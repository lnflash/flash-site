Cashu Provisioner: request data deletion

This page applies to Cashu Provisioner (Operator) on Android and Cashu Card Provisioner on iOS, published by Island Bitcoin.

How to make a request

  1. Email support@getflash.io with the subject Cashu Provisioner data deletion request.
  2. Include your operator username, your organization, and whether you want your operator access closed, personal data deleted, or both. You can make the request without opening or signing in to the app.
  3. Support will verify that you are authorized to make the request and explain what can be deleted and what must be retained. Do not send passwords, session tokens, card keys, PINs, PUKs, or recovery keys.

Data covered by your request

Requests can cover your operator account details and personal identifiers linked to your device, batches, and provisioning attempts. Support reviews which information can be deleted or de-identified. Closing operator access and deleting personal data are separate actions.

Records that may be retained

Card identities, installation and interruption records, key-recovery records, and security audit evidence may need to remain so that issued cards can be recovered, interrupted installations can be handled safely, and a card is not mistakenly reused. Records may also need to remain to meet applicable legal obligations.

These recovery and security records currently have no fixed automatic expiry. Support will explain which records are retained and why when handling your request. A deletion request does not automatically erase a card's recovery keys, quarantine record, or installation history.

Questions

Contact support@getflash.io. See the Flash Privacy Policy for general privacy information.
