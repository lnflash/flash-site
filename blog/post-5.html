Getting paid from abroad — and moving your money where you need it — has always been harder than it should be for people in the Caribbean. International clients need an easy way to send you money. Freelance platforms often ask for a US bank account. And once money arrives, moving it onward can take time. This release makes all of it simple.

Flash now puts two things in your pocket that used to be out of reach: your own international account to get paid, and the freedom to move your money any way you want.

Get paid from anywhere

Once you're verified, Flash gives you your own US or EUR account — a real account number and routing details, right inside your app.

That means anyone, anywhere can pay you exactly like they'd pay any US or European bank account:

  • International clients and customers paying your invoices
  • Employers and freelance platforms sending your wages
  • Family and friends abroad sending money home

No US address. No bank branch. No minimum balance. Just share your account details, and the money lands in Flash — ready to spend, hold, or move on.

Cash out, your way

Getting money in is only half the story. When it's time to take it out, Flash puts you in control. Cash out however suits you:

  • To a US bank account
  • To a EUR bank account
  • To your local bank — Jamaica and beyond
  • To another Flash wallet, instantly and free
  • To any Lightning wallet
  • To on-chain Bitcoin

One balance, and the choice is always yours. Your money moves the way you want it to.

Money should move as freely as people do — easy to receive, easy to hold, easy to send, wherever you are in the world.

Simple by design

Behind the scenes, Flash is built on Bitcoin and its Lightning Network — that's how money moves in seconds, across borders, for a fraction of the usual cost. But you never have to buy it, hold it, or understand it. Flash handles the technology so you don't have to.

And if you do want to hold or save in Bitcoin? That's one of your options too — entirely your choice. Getting paid and moving your money ask nothing technical of you. They simply work.

Built in the Caribbean. Open to the world.

Flash started in Jamaica, grew across the Caribbean, and now reaches around the world. The same rails that connect a family across islands connect a freelancer to a client an ocean away — the same access to global money, for everyone.
